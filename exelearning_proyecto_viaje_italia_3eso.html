eXeLearning: Project-Based Learning (3º ESO, Andalucía)

English Future Tenses Oral Presentation Travel to Italy

Project title: Our Future Trip to Italy
Group size: 4–5 students
Final product: slideshow + oral presentation (8–10 minutes)

1) Learning objectives

  • Use different future forms in context: be going to, will, future continuous, and present simple (for timetables).
  • Apply travel-related vocabulary accurately in written and oral tasks.
  • Design and present a realistic future trip to Italy collaboratively.
  • Improve pronunciation, fluency, and interaction during oral presentation.

2) Grammar focus (future forms)

Form Use Example for the project
Be going to Plans and intentions We are going to book a room near the city centre.
Will Predictions, decisions at the moment, promises I think we will travel by air because it is faster.
Future continuous Actions in progress at a specific future time At 9:00 a.m. on Monday, we will be visiting the Colosseum.
Present simple Schedules and fixed timetables Our flight leaves Málaga at 07:30 and arrives in Rome at 10:05.

4) Slide structure (mandatory)

Slide 1 – Team introduction: group members and roles (planner, budget manager, language checker, speaker coordinator, designer).
Slide 2 – Destination in Italy: city/cities, why you chose them, what you expect to see.
Slide 3 – Transport and timetable: by air/land/sea, departure and arrival times, ticket office/information desk details (present simple for schedules).
Slide 4 – Accommodation: where you are going to stay, how you will make a reservation / book a room.
Slide 5 – 3-day itinerary: what you will be doing each day (future continuous).
Slide 6 – Budget and packing: suitcase, luggage allowance, passport, seats, total cost.
Slide 7 – Cultural behaviour: how tourists should behave and respect local places.
Slide 8 – Final reflection: personal predictions and expectations with will.

5) Oral presentation guidelines

  • Duration: 8–10 minutes per group.
  • Every student must speak for at least 1 minute.
  • Use at least 2 examples of each future form in the presentation.
  • Use at least 15 target vocabulary items naturally.
  • Eye contact, clear voice, and teamwork are required.

6) Assessment rubric (0–10)

Criterion Excellent (2) Good (1) Needs improvement (0)
Use of future forms All 4 forms used correctly and naturally Some mistakes, but message clear Frequent errors, limited variety
Vocabulary 15+ words used accurately 8–14 words with minor mistakes Under 8 words / repeated misuse
Content quality Realistic, creative, well-organized itinerary Mostly clear, some gaps Disorganized or incomplete
Oral performance Fluent, confident, good pronunciation Understandable with pauses Hard to follow
Teamwork and participation All members contribute equally Minor imbalance One or two members dominate
